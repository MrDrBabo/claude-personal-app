Claude Personal

A private, single-user Google OAuth app. It lets the account owner's own Claude desktop app read the owner's own Gmail and Calendar, read-only.

Privacy policy

The app accesses only the account owner's data, read-only (gmail.readonly, calendar.readonly). Data is processed only on the owner's own device and in the owner's own Claude sessions. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No other users. Contact: via the GitHub profile MrDrBabo.
